# Art bible: The Unreliable Shopkeeper

1. **Style:** painted storybook apothecary. Inked outlines, brass-framed panels with gem studs and filigree corners, parchment for anything written by hand, soft bloom only on magic.
2. **Palette:** night `#1E1430` (backdrop) · plum `#3B2758` (panels) · brass `#D9A54E` (frames, currency) · parchment `#F3E4C4` (paper, dialogue) · ink `#2A1830` (outlines, text on paper) · magic accent `#C08CFF` (selection, brewing, discovery) · ember `#FF9A3C` (warm light, heat).
3. **Mood:** warm candle and cauldron light as the key; cool moonlight through the arched windows as fill; shadows stay plum, never black.
